Cookie & Storage Policy

Zero tracking cookies · Full transparency

1. No Tracking Cookies

ShapeMyImage does not use advertising cookies, marketing pixels, or invasive third-party trackers. We do not track your browsing history or follow you across websites.

2. Functional Theme Preference

To provide a pleasant user experience, we save exactly one setting on your device:

Item Where Stored Why It Is Used
theme Your browser storage Remembers whether you prefer Dark Mode or Light Mode so you don't have to switch it each visit.

This setting contains zero personal or identifiable information. You can clear it anytime directly from your browser settings.

3. Your Images Are Never Stored

Your uploaded photos and shape adjustments are held only in temporary working memory while you use the editor. No image data is ever written to cookies or tracking storage.

4. Managing Your Preferences

You can manage, inspect, or delete stored website data through your browser settings:

  • Chrome & Edge: Settings → Privacy & Security → Delete browsing data.
  • Safari: Settings → Privacy → Manage Website Data.
  • Firefox: Settings → Privacy & Security → Cookies and Site Data.
